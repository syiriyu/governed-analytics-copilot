# Governed Analytics Copilot

An experimental natural-language analytics application exploring how
semantic modelling and governance can improve the reliability of
LLM-generated business analytics.

## Business Scenario

Atlas Business Services is a fictional multi-region B2B organisation
providing subscription and usage-based services to corporate customers.

The business operates across multiple customer segments, products,
regions and account-management teams.

## Problem

Business users often rely on analysts or predefined dashboards to answer
questions from enterprise data.

Large language models create an opportunity for users to ask analytical
questions using natural language, but technically valid queries can
still produce incorrect business answers when metrics and terminology
are ambiguous.

## Objective

Build and evaluate a governed natural-language analytics system that
uses structured business metadata and semantic definitions to generate
reliable analytical queries.

## Core Question

To what extent does semantic grounding improve the accuracy and
reliability of natural-language business analytics?
